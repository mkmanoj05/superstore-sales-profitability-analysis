# superstore-sales-profitability-analysis
# Sales and profitability analysis using Excel, SQL and Tableau

**About the Project**

I worked on this project to understand the sales and profitability performance of a Superstore business using historical sales data.

I started by cleaning and exploring the data in Excel. After that, I used SQL to perform deeper analysis and answer different business questions. Finally, I used Tableau to visualize the results and make the findings easier to understand.

The main focus of the project was not just looking at sales, but also understanding which products, customers, categories and regions were contributing to profit and where the business could improve.

---

## Tools Used

* Excel
* SQL
* Tableau

## Dataset

The dataset contains information about orders, customers, products, sales, profit, discounts, shipping methods and geographic locations.

Some of the main columns used in the analysis include:

* Product Name

* Sales
* Profit
* Discount

* Segment
* City
* State
* Region

## What I Did

### 1. Data Cleaning & Initial Analysis – Excel

I first cleaned and explored the dataset in Excel.

Some of the work included:

* Checking the data for inconsistencies
* Preparing the data for analysis
* Exploring sales and profit trends
* Comparing different categories and segments
* Looking at product and regional performance

### 2. Data Analysis – SQL

After the initial analysis, I loaded the data into SQL and used queries to investigate different business questions.

Some of the analysis included:

* Monthly sales and profit
* Yearly revenue, profit and profit margin
* Sales and profit by shipping mode
* Top customers by profit
* Sales and profit by customer segment
* City and regional performance
* Category and sub-category performance
* Top products by sales
* Top products by profit
* Running revenue by month
* Discount and profitability analysis

I also used CTEs, aggregations and window functions while working with the data.

### 3. Visualization – Tableau

I created a Tableau dashboard to present the main findings from the analysis.

The dashboard focuses on sales performance, profitability, product performance and other important business metrics.

---

## Questions I Tried to Answer

* How are sales and profit changing over time?
* Which categories generate the most revenue?
* Which categories are the most profitable?
* Which products generate the highest sales?
* Which products generate the highest profit?
* Which customers contribute the most profit?
* Which regions and cities perform the best?
* How does discounting relate to profitability?
* Which areas of the business may need further investigation?

---

## Key Takeaways

The analysis showed that sales performance and profitability are not always the same.

Some products and areas can generate high revenue without generating equally strong profit. I also looked at discount levels to understand how discounting is associated with profitability.

These findings can help a business look beyond revenue and pay more attention to profit margins, product performance, customer contribution and regional performance.
